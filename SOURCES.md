# Sources — Saturday 21 November 2026

Every headline on the front page traces to a real story reported on 21 November 2026. Nothing on the image is invented.

1. **RUSSIAN MISSILES LAND IN POLAND** — "Two missiles and a drone crash in the south-east during a huge overnight bombardment of Ukraine. No one hurt. PM Tusk calls it an intentional provocation; NATO chief Rutte calls it a grave violation." / "Strikes on Ukraine kill at least eight. Austrian embassy in Kyiv hit."
   - Two Russian missiles (one a Kh-101 cruise missile) and a suspected jet-powered drone crashed in south-eastern Poland during a massive overnight Russian bombardment of Ukraine (400+ drones, 110+ missiles). No one hurt in Poland. Polish PM Donald Tusk called it an "intentional provocation" timed to coincide with European military exercises nearby; NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte called it a "grave violation" of Polish airspace and is due in Warsaw on 21 Nov. The strikes on Ukraine killed at least eight people (per President Zelenskyy); the Austrian embassy in Kyiv was hit and France called for a UN Security Council meeting.

Nvidia plunged 14% after Reuters reported the SEC is probing its accounting; AMD −13%, Micron −14%, Broadcom −12%. An AI-stock sell-off; Anthropic's IPO gave back its first-day gains.

3. **UK AND EU SIGN RESET DEALS** — "Burnham and von der Leyen seal food trade, emissions and youth mobility pacts. Youth scheme: 100,000 per side, ages 18 to 31, up to 3 years."
   - PM Andy Burnham (in office after Keir Starmer resigned; summit postponed from July) signed three deals with the EU in Brussels: easing food/agri trade (no certificates; UK says +£5.1bn a year), linking UK–EU emissions trading systems from 2028 (+£3.8bn a year), and a youth mobility scheme — 100,000 young people aged 18–31 per side, up to 3 years, by 2030. EU chief Ursula von der Leyen called it a "turning point". University tuition fees deferred for six months.

4. **TRUMP, XI AND PUTIN MEET** — "First trilateral summit in Shenzhen adopts a four-point declaration. Putin warns the response to Kerch Bridge strikes will be far more painful."
   - Trump, Xi and Putin held the first-ever trilateral meeting (90 minutes) at APEC in Shenzhen on 19 Nov and adopted a four-point declaration (nuclear security, energy, UN reform, international law). Putin then warned Russia's response to Ukraine's strikes on the Kerch Bridge would be "far more painful". The Trump administration is pressing for a Ukraine peace deal by early January (Financial Times); US envoy Jared Kushner said Moscow may accept NATO-style security guarantees.

5. **WORLD COURT: MYANMAR GUILTY OF GENOCIDE** — "ICJ rules 9-6 over the Rohingya. Reparations ordered."
   - The International Court of Justice ruled 9–6 on 19 Nov that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in its 2016–2017 operations — the first time the court has found a state responsible for genocide. Myanmar was ordered to pay reparations and allow the displaced Rohingya to return.

6. **EU GIVES UKRAINE 1 BILLION EUROS IN MILITARY AID** — "Delivery by end of 2027."
