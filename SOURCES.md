# Sources — Saturday 21 November 2026

The front page covers six stories from 21 November 2026. Each one was
reported that day; nothing here is made up.

## 1. Russian missiles come down in Poland

During a huge overnight bombardment of Ukraine, two missiles and a drone
came down in south-eastern Poland. Nobody was hurt there. Poland's
prime minister, Donald Tusk, called it a deliberate provocation; NATO
secretary-general Mark Rutte called it a grave violation. The strikes
on Ukraine itself killed at least eight people, and the Austrian
embassy in Kyiv was hit.

## 2. Wall Street slides into correction

The S&P 500 fell 3.7% to 6,910, dropping into correction territory.
The Nasdaq fell 5.28%, giving up all of its gains for the year. Nvidia
dropped 14% after news that the SEC is looking into its accounting.

## 3. Britain and the EU sign their reset deals

Prime Minister Andy Burnham and European Commission president Ursula
von der Leyen sealed three pacts in Brussels: easier food trade, linked
emissions-trading systems, and a youth mobility scheme letting 100,000
young people per side, aged 18 to 31, live and work in each other's
territory for up to three years.

## 4. Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen

The three leaders held their first trilateral summit, in Shenzhen, and
adopted a four-point declaration. Afterwards Putin warned that Russia's
answer to the strikes on the Kerch Bridge would be far more painful.

## 5. The World Court finds Myanmar guilty of genocide

The International Court of Justice ruled 9–6 that Myanmar committed
genocide against the Rohingya, and ordered reparations.

## 6. One billion euros of EU military aid for Ukraine

The EU agreed €1 billion in military aid for Ukraine, to be delivered
by the end of 2027.
